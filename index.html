Chapter 04 • Proof & Real Numbers
Featured Case Study Unreal Engine 5 • Grand Strategy / RTS

How Broken Rampart Interactive Turned a Viral Prototype Into a Bankable $5.06M Publisher Package

Broken Rampart Interactive is an independent European team of 6 specialists, including an ex-NVIDIA GPU optimization architect, software engineers, and a medieval historian, building Project Renaissance, an ambitious medieval grand strategy and real-time tactics game in Unreal Engine 5 capable of rendering 10,000 to 40,000 soldiers at 60 to 80 FPS.

After strategy YouTuber STRATMAN covered their early prototype, the studio organically generated 600,000+ YouTube views (140,000+ on the first showcase video alone) and a 1,300+ member Discord community. Tier-1 strategy publishers (Hooded Horse and MicroProse) opened talks. Before wiring $5.06M USD across a 36-month timeline, they needed a bulletproof production roadmap and P&L.

01. The Diligence Bottlenecks

Despite extraordinary technical and community traction, the studio faced three critical diligence risks:

  • • Unbounded Pre-Funding Scope: The art pipeline scoped 400 armors (300 unique) and 800 medieval buildings, while engineering planned to build unstarted Campaign AI and 8-player multiplayer before funding.
  • • A Costly P&L Tax Error: Their financial sheet deducted 20% corporate tax from gross Steam revenue before recouping the $5.06M development budget.
  • • Missing Production Governance: Scaling from 6 part-timers to 15 to 19 full-time developers required veteran AA/AAA production leadership.

02. What We Built Together

  • • Scoped a 30-Min Scripted Vertical Slice: Locked the pre-funding demo to one playable historical scenario proving the 40k-unit battle engine, deferring Campaign AI and multiplayer to funded Alpha milestones.
  • • 3-Part Publisher Pitch Architecture: Separated materials into a visual Teaser, a 15-slide Sales Deck, and a 20-page Diligence Pre-Read with a full Risk Register.
  • • Competitive Cost Benchmarking: Analyzed official UK Companies House filings for The Creative Assembly (£98.13M annual dev spend across 800 staff) to prove Broken Rampart could deliver a Total War-class experience for 95% less capital.

03. The Quantified Impact

  • • -20% Lower Break-Even Threshold: Rebuilding the tax and recoup waterfall reduced the publisher’s break-even point by 63,250 copies, dropping from 316,250 units down to 253,000 units at a $40 SRP (50/50 split while keeping 100% of their IP).
  • • +76% Higher Studio Net Profit (+$507K USD): At the baseline target of 400,000 units sold ($8.0M net post-Steam revenue), projected studio net profit rose from $669,000 to $1,176,000 USD.
  • • Co-Signed Production Leadership: Joined the pitch package as named Production Advisor / Fractional EP (PMP®, Ex-CIG) for ongoing publisher diligence.
