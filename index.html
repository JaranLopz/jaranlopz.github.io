03 / Featured Case Study & Verified Proof

Structuring a $5.06M USD AA Strategy Pitch for Hooded Horse & MicroProse

How Broken Rampart Interactive transformed an organic viral prototype (600K+ YouTube views) into an institutional-grade 36-month production and financial package.

Target Development Budget $5.06M USD 36-Month Roadmap • 6 to 19 FTEs
Break-Even Threshold -20% Units Reduced by 63,250 copies (316k → 253k)
Studio Net Profit Protected +$507K (+76%) $669K → $1.18M at 400K units sold
Pre-Pitch Traction 600K+ Views 140K YouTube debut + 1,300 Discord

The Challenge: Deep Technical Talent, Zero Production Packaging

Project Renaissance is an ambitious Unreal Engine 5 historical grand strategy/RTS game (1066 to 1550) built by a 6-person technical team including an ex-NVIDIA GPU optimization architect capable of rendering 10,000 to 40,000 soldiers at 60 to 80 FPS. However, before approaching strategy publishers, their Vertical Slice was over-scoped, their 300-unit armor pipeline lacked dependency tracking, and their P&L spreadsheet contained a critical tax-before-recoup formula error.

What We Built Together:

  • 01 Vertical Slice De-Scoping: Cut unstarted Campaign AI and 8-player multiplayer from the pre-funding build, locking a tight 30-minute scripted battle loop to prove USPs without burning out the core engineers.
  • 02 3-Tier Pitch Package: Separated an overcrowded presentation into a 5-slide visual Teaser, a 15-slide live Sales Deck, and a comprehensive 20-page Diligence Pre-Read.
  • 03 P&L Recoupment Correction: Fixed a formula deducting 20% corporate tax on gross post-Steam revenue prior to recouping the $5.06M development budget, lowering break-even by 20% and increasing studio net profit at 400k copies by +76% (+$507,000 USD).
  • 04 Statutory Competitor Benchmarking: Extracted UK Companies House statutory financial filings for The Creative Assembly Limited (£112.28M turnover, £98.13M dev spend across 800 staff) to prove lean AA unit-economics.
