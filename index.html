The Funding Bottleneck

Why Tier-1 Publishers Pass on Great Game Prototypes

Having a playable Unreal or Unity build and organic YouTube traction gets you a first meeting. Passing publisher due diligence takes operational maturity.

Red Flag #1

Unproven Scope & Asset Math

You know what game you want to build, but you can’t prove how 12 developers will deliver 300+ unique 3D assets, complex AI, and 60 FPS optimization in 30 months without burning out.

Red Flag #2

Broken Financial & Unit Economics

Your budget is a rough guess. Publishers immediately spot missing tax models, unrealistic recoup timelines, and sales projections that aren't backed by SteamDB or Boxleiter market comps.

Red Flag #3

No Senior Production Governance

Publishers rarely hand $1M–$5M to talented engineers and creatives who have never managed a full production cycle. They need an experienced Producer in the room protecting their investment.
