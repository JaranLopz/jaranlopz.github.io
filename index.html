The problem

Why pitches stall in review

Most strong prototypes fail at the same three points.

Scope that can’t ship

Pre-funding plans include unstarted systems, very large asset counts and multiplayer nobody has budgeted for.

Financial models that break

Tax taken before recoupment, platform fees left out, or headcount that doesn’t match the roadmap.

No experienced producer

Publishers and investors want to see who will deliver the project on time and on budget.

Proof

Case study: a publisher-ready package for a $5M strategy game

Broken Rampart Interactive · Project Renaissance (Unreal Engine 5, grand strategy)
Needed a plan that could pass publisher review before committing $5.06M over 36 months.

  • Narrowed scope. Locked a 30-minute vertical slice and moved unstarted systems to funded milestones.
  • Fixed the money model. Rebuilt the tax and recoupment order. The publisher’s break-even dropped from 316,250 to 253,000 units at a $40 SRP.
  • Improved the studio’s outlook. Projected net profit at the 400,000-unit baseline rose from $669K to $1.176M.
  • Gave the pitch a producer. Joined the package as production advisor for ongoing publisher diligence.
