06 • Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a Fractional Operations Manager different from a Virtual Assistant? +

A Virtual Assistant is great when you already have a clear checklist of tasks and just need someone to execute instructions. As a Fractional Operations Manager, I partner with you at a higher level: helping you decide what to prioritize, designing the workflow, spotting risks before they become problems, and owning the follow-through to completion.

Do I need to work in video games or tech to work with you? +

Not at all. While my background includes AAA and indie game production, the core challenges of running a business—prioritizing goals, organizing client pipelines, coordinating people, and shipping projects on time—are universal across coaching, consulting, creative agencies, and service businesses.

How do monthly retainer hours work? +

Retainers reserve dedicated weekly capacity for your business (typically 5 or 10 hours per week on a 4- to 6-month agreement) billed at a predictable monthly rate. If you ever have a major launch or event that requires extra hours beyond your retainer, we simply agree on it in advance.

Will you force me to switch all my software tools? +

Never. I adapt to the tools you already use—whether that’s Notion, Trello, ClickUp, Asana, Jira, Google Workspace, HubSpot, or a simple spreadsheet—and only suggest changes when a tool is actively slowing you down.
